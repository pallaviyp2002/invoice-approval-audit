Audit Finding — High Severity

The invoice approval control for payments exceeding ₹1,00,000 is not operating effectively. Control testing of the full population of 138 high-value invoices identified 24 failures (17.4%) — more than three times the tolerable threshold of 5%. The failure rate is statistically significant at p < 0.0001.

Financial exposure from unapproved high-value invoices totals ₹46,85,893. The HR department recorded the highest failure count (7 invoices), followed by Finance and Operations (6 each). No evidence of a system-enforced approval hold was identified.

Recommendation

Implement a system-level payment block on all invoices exceeding ₹1,00,000 until a Level-2 digital approval is received and logged. Introduce a monthly dashboard review by the Finance Controller. Backdate a manual approval check for all 24 failed invoices.
